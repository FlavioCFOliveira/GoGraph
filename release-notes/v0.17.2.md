# Release Notes — GoGraph v0.17.2

**Release date:** 2026-10-13 (pending the tag)
**Tag:** `v0.17.2`
**Previous release:** [`v0.17.1`](v0.17.1.md) (2026-10-13)
**Module path:** `github.com/FlavioCFOliveira/GoGraph`
**Go toolchain:** `go.mod` pins `go 1.26` and `toolchain go1.27.2`.
`go.mod` and `go.sum` are byte-identical at `v0.17.1` and `18ec0dc7`: no dependency moved in
this window.

## Read this first

**`v0.17.2` is a drop-in PATCH release. Embedders pinned to `v0.17.1` or earlier should move
their pin to `v0.17.2`.**

1. **A write statement cut by its context deadline now rolls back in linear time.** Up to
   `v0.17.1`, the rollback deleted every node the statement had created one at a time and copied
   the whole tombstone bitmap for each: the rollback was quadratic. A statement cut at 1 s
   returned 8.6 to 9.2 s after its deadline; it now returns about 1.4 s after it. See
   [Fixes](#fixes).
2. **No exported API change and no format change.** No exported identifier was added, removed
   or re-signed; no WAL, snapshot or other on-disk format changed. A store written by `v0.17.1`
   opens unchanged, and `v0.17.1` can still open a store written by `v0.17.2`.
3. **No behaviour change beyond latency and memory.** The rollback reaches the same end state as
   before; only its duration and its peak heap change.

## Fixes

### A cut write statement rolls back in linear time (rmp #3122, #3123)

Present in `v0.17.1` and earlier. The rollback of a write statement cut by its context deadline
replayed its undo log through the ordinary node delete. That path copied the whole copy-on-write
tombstone bitmap once per node, and recorded a death, an exclusive adjacency claim and write-set
entries that the abort then withdrew again. The rollback was quadratic. A
`MATCH (a), (b), (c) CREATE ()` over 600 nodes cut at 1 s returned 8.6 to 9.2 s after its
deadline and peaked at about 1.7 GB of heap; cut at 5 s it returned 64 s late and peaked at
7.2 GB.

The abort now withdraws the nodes the statement created itself, and flips their tombstones in
batches, with the same end state (`b1e9e21f`). A node qualifies only when its whole life record
is one birth by the aborting transaction, with no death, no existence claim and an empty label
bag; every other node keeps the physical removal. Each batch of tombstone flips is published by
one atomic store, so a reader sees either none or all of a batch.

The same statement now returns about 1.4 s after a 1 s deadline with a peak of about 0.75 GB,
and about 7.1 s after a 5 s deadline with a peak of about 3 GB. The deadline itself was always
noticed within microseconds; the whole overrun was the rollback.

### The abort path allocates about a third less per created node (rmp #3125)

The withdrawal lists are pre-sized instead of grown by append; a duplicate unborn-mark pass is
skipped; and a batch of at least 1 024 ids flips or clears tombstones with one bitmap `Or` or
`AndNot` instead of per-id updates (`2885d7d3`). Smaller batches keep the per-id path. Rolling
back a 5 s cut `CREATE` allocates 197 B per id instead of the 307 B measured after `b1e9e21f`
alone.

A large `DELETE` rollback was measured linear and is unchanged (rmp #3124).

## Changes by area

The complete list is the `## [0.17.2]` entry of [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

- **LPG engine:** abort withdraws statement-created nodes instead of replaying their removal;
  tombstone flips and releases on abort are applied per batch; withdrawal lists are pre-sized.
- **Tests:** `cypher/rollback_bounded_3122_test.go` adds two short-layer regression gates,
  `TestCutCreateReturnsPromptly_3122` (overrun bounded by 2.5 times the deadline) and
  `TestCutCreateRollbackPeakHeap_3123` (rollback peak heap bounded by 1.5 times the heap at the
  deadline).
- **Project documentation:** the development-workflow instructions now require the rmp comment
  log as the work-tracking record, written at the moment and place each event occurs.

## Behaviour changes you can observe

- A write statement cut by its context deadline returns sooner and holds less heap while it
  rolls back. The state it leaves behind is unchanged.

## Known limits

- **Rollback remains linear in the work done:** about 1 µs per created node. A statement cut
  at its deadline still returns late in proportion to the nodes it created: about 1.4 s after a
  1 s deadline, about 7.1 s after a 5 s deadline.
- The `v0.17.1` cost of releasing minted keys on abort (about 1.5 s per about 1.6 M released
  ids) was not re-measured for this release.

## Upgrade

**Upgrade is drop-in.** Change the version in `go.mod`:

```bash
go get github.com/FlavioCFOliveira/GoGraph@v0.17.2
```

No code change, no migration and no store rewrite is needed. A store written by `v0.17.1` opens
as is; the WAL and snapshot formats are those of `v0.17.1`, so a downgrade to `v0.17.1` remains
possible. Embedders still on `v0.15.0` or `v0.16.0` must also apply the migration notes of
[`v0.16.0`](v0.16.0.md) and [`v0.17.0`](v0.17.0.md).

## Compliance mandates

- **openCypher TCK:** `tckExecutionBaseline` is 3 897 and no feature file changed.
- **ACID:** the abort reaches the same end state as before. A withdrawn node's birth record stays
  in flight until the abort marks it aborted, so no snapshot reader can see it, and a writer that
  reaches it meets a conflict, as during the statement. Committed work is untouched.
- **Efficiency:** the release removes quadratic work and per-node garbage from rollback; see
  [Performance](#performance).
- **Security:** no toolchain or dependency change.

## Test evidence summary

**The release gate is `VERSION=v0.17.2 make release-preflight`** (release-accuracy plus `make ci`).
Its result is recorded by the releaser at the tag; this file does not claim it.

**Regression gates added by the release** run in the short layer, and therefore in `make ci`:
`TestCutCreateReturnsPromptly_3122` and `TestCutCreateRollbackPeakHeap_3123`. Each lengthens its
deadline until one cut statement has drawn a fixed minimum of node ids, so it judges the same
amount of work on a fast host and under `-race`. The release-preparation commit changes
documentation only.

**Not established by this file:** the release-preflight result at the tag, the sprint-close gate
result, `make test-crashinject`, `make cover-gate`, the soak test and any load test.
