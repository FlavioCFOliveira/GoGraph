Which benchmarks make -test.cpu=1,8,64,256,1024 a REAL GOROUTINE LADDER
==============================================================================================

-test.cpu only builds a goroutine ladder for a benchmark that creates concurrency ITSELF.
On a serial benchmark it varies GOMAXPROCS and nothing else: no concurrent reader, no
contention, no scaling curve.

Two checks, because either one alone misclassifies:
  * b.RunParallel(...)                      -- the usual form
  * a hand-rolled `go func` behind a WaitGroup -- e.g. store/txn BenchmarkCommitConcurrent,
    which drives genuinely concurrent commits and would be misfiled as serial by a
    RunParallel-only screen.
A NAME is not evidence either way.
