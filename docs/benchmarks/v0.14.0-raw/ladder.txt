THE CONCURRENCY LADDER — 1 / 8 / 64 / 256 / 1024 goroutines
Only benchmarks that CREATE concurrency themselves are here (see concurrency-classification.txt).
Under b.RunParallel, b.N is the TOTAL iteration count across all goroutines, so ns/op is
INVERSE AGGREGATE THROUGHPUT, not per-goroutine latency.
Scaling is each arm against ITS OWN level-1 cell, never a raw cross-level number.
