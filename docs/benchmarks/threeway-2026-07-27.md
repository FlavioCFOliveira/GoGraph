# Three-way head-to-head: GoGraph vs Neo4j vs Memgraph — 2026-07-27 (round 4)

Re-measurement at `bd3f1a38`, after the sprint 316–325 remediation of the round-3 audit.
Directly comparable with [`threeway-2026-07-26.md`](threeway-2026-07-26.md): same harness
(`bench/comparison/threeway_test.go`, build tag `threeway`), same scale, same dataset, same
dialect overrides.

- Apple M4 (10 cores, 32 GB), Neo4j 5.26 Community and Memgraph 2.22.0 under Docker/colima,
  GoGraph in-process.
- `THREEWAY_NODES=20000` → 20 000 nodes / 199 941 edges. Median of 9 after 3 warm-ups.
- Row counts cross-checked across all three targets before any timing was compared: identical
  for every query (see the cross-check table below).

**Read this alongside the two methodological caveats in
[`../audit-vs-neo4j-memgraph-2026-07-27.md`](../audit-vs-neo4j-memgraph-2026-07-27.md) §5:**
the GoGraph target is a bare in-memory `lpg.Graph` with **no WAL and no fsync**, while Neo4j
forces its transaction log per commit and Memgraph writes a WAL (fsyncing every 100 000
transactions by default). The write figures are therefore not durability-comparable; the read
figures understate GoGraph's deficit, because it carries no durability cost at all.
